## Reasoning

Well the website is now up and running. I like it a lot. But I thought to myself "one color palette is great, but why not two? or more?" so I did exactly that. I've added 2 more color profiles that can be accessed by clicking the button in the far bottom left corner.

At this point in time, the available color palettes are Persona 3, Persona 4 and Persona 5. This also became a challenge to me, that is to design a website that is modular and highly customizable without hardcoding values everywhere.

Alongside color profile changes, I've also added sound effects throughout this website, so the whole experience is more dynamic. Speaking of experience, now users are able to scroll through the menus by clicking "A" or going to top with "B", simulating a joystick albeit some limitations still occur. Will be fixed soon though!

Overall, very happy with how it turned out. Next I'm gonna direct my attention to other high values project. And I'm going to keep updating here. Thanks for reading.
